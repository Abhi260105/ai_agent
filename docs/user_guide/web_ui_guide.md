Widgets
Available Widgets:

My Tasks: Tasks assigned to you
Recent Activity: Team activity feed
My Projects: Projects you're involved in
Upcoming Deadlines: Tasks due soon
Project Progress: Completion charts
Time Tracking: Hours logged this week
Notifications: Unread notifications
Quick Actions: Common shortcuts

Customize Dashboard:

Click "Customize" in top-right
Drag widgets to rearrange
Click "+" to add widgets
Click "×" on widget to remove
Click "Save Layout"
